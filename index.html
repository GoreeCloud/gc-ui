DESIGN THAT HOLDS UP

Beautiful is usable.

Our expressive moments are always balanced by clarity, comfort, inclusivity, and performance.

01

Content leads

Canvas and Surface remain readable and stable. Reserve Lens for floating actions and navigation.

02

Contrast is deliberate

Text meets contrast targets against its effective background, including when material effects change.

03

Motion explains

Transitions convey hierarchy or state. Reduced-motion preferences remove nonessential movement.

04

Every input counts

Keyboard focus, pointer controls, touch targets, labels, and native semantics receive equal care.

05

Adaptive, not miniature

Change navigation and content composition to match the available screen, instead of shrinking a desktop layout.

06

Identity stays original

GC UI draws lessons from established platforms but owns its vocabulary, design decisions, and implementation.
